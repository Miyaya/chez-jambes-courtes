# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Chinese-diaspora foodies in France (bilingual 中文/FR). They're nostalgic for
Taiwanese fruit — fully-ripened mangoes, fragrant lychees, intense red-flesh
dragon fruit — that isn't sold in ordinary supermarkets. They need reassurance
that the fruit is authentic, air-freighted fresh, and worth paying a premium
for despite arriving in strictly limited batches.

## Product Purpose

Chez Jambes Courtes imports rare, fully-ripe Taiwanese fruit by air in small,
limited batches. The storefront (checkout, preorder, instock) is not yet
public — right now the site's whole job is converting visitors into
subscribers on the notify list (`/s-abonner`) for the next drop. Success today
means subscriber growth, not completed purchases.

## Brand Personality

Playful and artisanal — a friendly local market stall, not a slick storefront.
Three words: **playful, artisanal, hand-picked**. The bilingual FR/中文 framing
is deliberate and not privileging either language.

## Anti-references

- Generic slick SaaS/DTC storefront: no cold corporate gradient-heroes, no
  stock-photo genericness — it should feel handmade and specific to this
  business, not templated.
- Traditional-Chinese pastiche: no red-and-gold lantern/dragon clichés. The
  brand is playful/artisanal, not a "Chinese restaurant" visual trope.

## Design Principles

1. **Design for the notify list, not the cart.** Every storefront surface
   right now should optimize for subscribing, not for browsing/buying —
   checkout and product flows are not yet public.
2. **Both languages, one voice.** FR and 中文 are always paired, never one
   as a translation afterthought of the other.
3. **Scarcity as craft, not hype.** Limited-batch, air-imported fruit is the
   premium story — communicate it with specificity (real fruit names, real
   constraints) rather than urgency-marketing tropes (countdown timers,
   fake stock warnings).
4. **Handmade, not templated.** Avoid the generic DTC/SaaS visual grammar;
   lean into the market-stall, hand-picked feel already present in the
   organic blob illustrations and marquee.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal accessibility target set beyond standard practice; follow WCAG AA
conventions (contrast, keyboard access, reduced-motion) as a baseline.
